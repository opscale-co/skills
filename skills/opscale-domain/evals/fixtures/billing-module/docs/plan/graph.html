▶SP-01-03Issue the invoice Validates the totals and moves the invoice to Issued.step
▶SP-01-03-L01issue-invoice [BR-01]logic
▶issue-invoiceIssue invoice SIPOClogic

Validates the invoice totals and moves it from Draft to Issued.

Inputs

  • invoice options · required — Draft invoice to issue

Process

  • SP-01-03-L01-C01 — Sets the total, the issue moment and the Issued status [BR-01]

Outputs

  • success bool — Whether the invoice was issued
  • invoice invoice — The issued invoice

Business rules

  • BR-01 — Only a Draft invoice with at least one item can be issued; its total is the sum of its line totals.

Soft failures

  • The invoice has no items

Hard failures

  • The invoice does not exist or is not Draft
