SP-01 Invoicing
Trigger: an operator needs to bill a customer
Ends with: wait for the customer's payment
01) Register the customer
02) Create the invoice with its items
03) Issue the invoice (validates totals, sets status Issued)

SP-02 Settlement
Trigger: the wait from Invoicing resolved (payment arrived)
Ends with: —
01) Record the payment (sets status Paid)
